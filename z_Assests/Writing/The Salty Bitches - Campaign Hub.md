# The Salty Bitches

*A crew of sailors, thieves, druids, paladins, and one very old tortle who met in a plague-struck port and sailed to the edge of the world together.*

---

## The Crew

### [[Vel'Korr]]
*Drow rogue — Captain*

A Drow pirate raised by a morally gray band of pirates called [[The Red Talons]]. Charming, dangerous, and driven by a vendetta against the wizard who destroyed everything he loved. He arrived in [[Duskwind Landing]] looking for coin and left with a purpose. He departed at the end of the campaign aboard the Red Talon vessel, bound for [[The Underdark]] with [[Zora Meadowlark]].

---

### [[Faefi]]
*Aasimar cleric/barbarian — Master of Arms*

A storm of barely contained divine fury raised in the [[Abbey of the Silver Veil]] high in the [[Agite Mountains]]. The wizard [[Viros]] destroyed that abbey and took [[Mother Elyra]] — the closest thing to a mother Faefi had ever known. She came down from the mountains and found [[Vel'Korr]] in the rubble, and together they hunted Viros across the world. She departed with Vel'Korr at the end, heading into the underdark.

---

### [[Picasso]]
*Tortle warlock — First Mate*

A very old tortle with a rat named [[Sliver]] and a pact with something vast and ancient at the bottom of the ocean. [[Cthulhu]] had been reaching out to him since before the campaign began. By the end, Picasso answered — painting the entity onto a dead dragon's skull with magic ink and giving it a foothold in the world.

---

### [[Kel]]
*Half-orc bard/paladin — Boatswain*

The son of the innkeepers who ran [[The Side Quest Tavern]] in [[Duskwind Landing]]. A builder and protector who finds meaning in [[Forgas]]'s philosophy — that there is something sacred in making things. He lost the tavern but kept the craft. He found his parents' porridge recipe on a bar at the edge of the world, and understood that what people make outlives them.

---

### [[Thorne]]
*Half-elf ranger — Angler*

From [[Heaven's Hole]], on her [[One-trek]] — the journey the commune sends its people on when they are ready to know the world. Thorne had a chaotic, buzzing energy and mysterious ties to the [[Feywild]]. She killed a void-corrupted sea dragon with a magic bean and a sixty-foot pyramid. She did not regret it.

---

### [[Soffalie]]
*Half-elf druid — Quartermaster*

Raised in [[Duskwind Landing]]'s [[Driftwood Forest]], which was destroyed in the third session of her adventure. She carried that loss across the whole campaign. She brought the [[wok-wok|Wok-woks]] aboard, who had very strong opinions about everything. She was there for all of it.

---

## Key Figures

### [[Viros]]
The pale elven wizard who started all of it. He destroyed [[The Red Talons]], raided the [[Abbey of the Silver Veil]], and spent the campaign building toward a ritual at [[Coffin Rock]]. He was killed at Coffin Rock when [[Faefi]] pushed him into the excavation pit he had dug himself.

### [[Captain Bubblebeard]]
Captain of [[The Stray Dogs]] and the most likable pirate the crew encountered. He hid the [[Tidemother]] Gate Stone somewhere safe and promised to take them to it in the morning. He was found dead in his chair before morning came. His glass eye was missing. The stone was not found with him.

### [[Zora Meadowlark]]
A Drow necromancer the crew pursued across the entire world before realising she was chasing the same thing they were. She wanted the Gate Stones to slow the spread of [[Void Rot]] and save her sister [[Sibil Meadowlark|Sibil]] and their homeland, [[The Underdark]]. She and [[Sibil]] fought alongside the crew at Coffin Rock. [[Vel'Korr]] and [[Faefi]] left with her afterward.

### [[Darla Murdinthe]]
Keeper of [[Whelk's Landing]], the neutral bar beneath the waterline at [[The Seat of Thalmyre]]. She served [[Sea-folk]] calm and Agithian Ale in equal measure. When the festival emptied and [[Bubblebeard]] was found dead, she was already behind the bar. *"Take what you need and go fix it. Whatever it is."*

### [[Mother Elyra]]
Leader of the [[Abbey of the Silver Veil]] and the closest thing [[Faefi]] had to a mother. Taken by [[Viros]] and used as a controlled instrument for most of the campaign. She was found on the Red Talon vessel after Viros's death, staring out at the water. She turned when [[Faefi]] called her name. She said she shouldn't have been there.

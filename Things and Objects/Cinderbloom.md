# [[Cinderbloom]]

Cinderbloom crystals catch the light like trapped embers — iridescent orange and deep red, warm to the touch even hours after being pulled from the earth.

## Description

[[Cinderbloom]] is a crystalline mineral that forms in the superheated fissures of [[Dracofold]]'s volcanic vents. Where magma cools slowly against pockets of trapped mineral-rich gas, Cinderbloom clusters grow in jagged, fragile formations — luminous and unmistakable. When ground into powder and burned as incense, or dissolved and drunk as a diluted nectar, it produces a warm euphoric haze. Users describe a loosening of the body, a brightening of colors, and a dreamlike sense of well-being that fades over several hours.

Regular use builds tolerance. Heavy use clouds the mind over months.

## Harvesting

Cinderbloom grows deepest where the volcanic heat is most intense. Harvesting requires workers to descend into active fissures — narrow passages where the air shimmers and long exposure destroys the lungs. Most harvesters develop a rasping cough within their first year. Few survive beyond five.

The humanoid vassal kingdoms under [[Tiamat]]'s chromatic dragon lords provide the labor: indentured workers, prisoners, and the dispossessed. The dragons take the yield as tribute; [[Tiamat]] sells the surplus through outside channels.

## Trade

[[Cinderbloom]] is smuggled out of [[Dracofold]] by [[The Murky Bellows]], who move it through the isles of [[Thalmyre]] before distributing it across [[Korlornium]]. The primary market is the wealthy elite of [[Cragbrook]], where it is consumed at private gatherings under the protection of corrupt civic officials who ensure no questions are asked about its origins.

## Cultural Significance — The Ashen Communion

The [[Dragonborn]] peoples of [[Dracofold]] consider [[Cinderbloom]] sacred. For generations they practiced the **Ashen Communion** — a ritual held at the close of each season in which communities gathered to drink diluted Cinderbloom nectar together. The ritual marked the turning of time, honored draconic ancestry, and was believed to reconnect the drinker to the fire that flows in [[Dragonborn]] blood. It was a moment of stillness in a continent defined by fear and labor.

[[Tiamat]] outlawed the Ashen Communion when she commercialized the harvest. Possession of Cinderbloom outside of approved channels in [[Dracofold]] is punishable by death.

The practice continues in secret among [[Dragonborn]] diaspora communities elsewhere in [[Erodas]]. It is rumored that small communities within [[Thalmyre]] still hold the Communion quietly — an irony not lost on those who know where the Cinderbloom they drink was shipped from.

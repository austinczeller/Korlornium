# Gods and Higher Powers

![[MassiveGod.jpeg]]

The divine powers of Erodas are ancient, vast forces whose will shapes nations, ecologies, and the very nature of magic across the known world. Little is understood about their true nature — most knowledge of the gods comes filtered through the faiths that serve them.

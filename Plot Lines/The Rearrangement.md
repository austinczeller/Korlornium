# [[The Rearrangement]]

> [!warning] DM Only
> The contents of this note are secret. The prophecy itself is known among the Star Touched, but the Elder Council's response to it — and their methods — are not public knowledge.

## Summary

The Star Touched of [[Goghdor]] have long held a prophecy: a Star Touched individual of immeasurable power will one day rearrange the constellations, unmaking the celestial order and throwing [[Erodas]] into profound imbalance. This event is called **The Rearrangement**.

The [[Elder Council of Goghdor]] takes this prophecy literally and acts on it in secret. Every Star Touched who completes the **Ascent** is observed. The nature of their [[Skoldrún]] — its species, its brightness, its behavior — is catalogued and assessed by council observers positioned near the summit. Most are deemed no threat. Those whose gifts suggest the capacity to reshape the cosmos on a large scale are quietly **eliminated** before they can develop their power further.

The council considers this a necessary and sorrowful duty. They do not enjoy it. They believe, completely, that they are saving the world.

## What the Players Know

The prophecy of The Rearrangement is publicly known among Star Touched — it is taught to children, spoken of by elders, and referenced in the context of the [[The Astral Volley]]. The existence of the Elder Council is also public. Their role as keepers of tradition and observers of the Ascent is openly acknowledged.

**What is secret:** That the Council eliminates candidates. Disappearances after the Ascent are explained as deaths from the climb, the cold, or the rare dangerous Skoldrún manifestation. No one outside the Council knows the true pattern.

## What is Actually Happening

The Council has been operating this way for centuries. Their records — kept in a sealed archive somewhere on the upper mountain — document every elimination and the gifts that triggered it. Over time, the criteria have shifted; what once required obvious world-altering power now catches individuals of merely exceptional ability. The Council has grown afraid, and fear has made their threshold lower.

A question held by only the oldest members, never spoken aloud: **the prophecy does not say why the constellations are rearranged.** It does not name the individual as villain or saviour. It says only that it will happen, and that the world will change. Whether the one who rearranges the sky is the cause of the imbalance — or the last attempt to prevent something worse — is unknown. The Council has never resolved this. They continue eliminating candidates anyway.

## Next Beat

The Rearrangement is dormant until the party encounters a Star Touched individual, hears rumors of disappearances after the Ascent, or travels to [[Goghdor]] directly.

# [[Crown of Scales]]

## Overview

The [[Crown of Scales]] is the ruling draconic authority of [[Dracofold]], the power structure through which [[Tiamat]] controls the continent and extends her economic reach into the wider world. It is less a traditional faction and more an imperial hierarchy — chromatic dragons serve as regional lords, humanoid vassal kingdoms pay tribute below them, and [[Tiamat]] sits at the apex of it all.

## Goals & Methods

The [[Crown of Scales]] seeks to consolidate draconic dominance over [[Dracofold]] and grow its wealth through trade rather than open warfare. The [[Cinderbloom]] export trade is its primary instrument — mined by humanoid labor under brutal conditions and distributed through outside partners like [[The Murky Bellows]].

The Crown suppresses cultural practices that undermine control, most notably the [[Dragonborn]] **Ashen Communion**, which [[Tiamat]] banned when she commercialized the [[Cinderbloom]] harvest.

## Structure

- **[[Tiamat]]** — supreme ruler, the mother dragon, first born of the [[Cradle of Scales]]
- **Chromatic Dragon Lords** — regional governors, each controlling a territory and its humanoid population
- **Vassal Kingdoms** — humanoid communities who pay tribute in labor and resources in exchange for protection and marginal autonomy

## Relationship to the Party

Not yet encountered directly. The Crown's influence reaches [[Korlornium]] through the [[Cinderbloom]] trade.

# [[Jorgen Gary Jehiamis Fisher|Jorgen "Gary" Jehiamis Fisher]]

Nobody can pronounce Jehiamis on the first try. Most clients don't make it past Jorgen. At some point a wealthy merchant from [[Dithoria City]] just started calling him Gary, and it stuck. He doesn't mind. Gary books more charters than Jorgen ever did.

## Description

[[Jorgen Gary Jehiamis Fisher|Gary]] is a broad, sun-weathered [[Human]] with a deeply tanned face, a wide jaw permanently fixed somewhere between a grin and a threat, and eyes the colour of shallow reef water. He is loud in the way that only people who are completely comfortable with themselves are loud — a laugh that carries across the dock, a handshake that could crack a walnut. He smells of salt, [[Sand Gulper]], and whatever the clients have been drinking.

He is the captain of the [[Gatorpaw Gang]] and the most hospitable pirate in all of [[Thalmyre]].

## The Tours

Gary runs bespoke fishing charters out of [[Gulper Island]] for high-paying clients — wealthy merchants, Dithorian nobles, and the occasional foreign dignitary who has heard that the [[Sand Gulper]] is the finest eating in [[Korlornium]] and wants to catch one themselves. The tours are expensive, expertly run, and genuinely excellent. Gary knows these waters better than anyone alive and he knows how to make a rich person feel like an adventurer without putting them in any actual danger.

Clients leave with a story, a mounted fish, and a standing invitation to return. Many do.

What they don't leave with is any awareness that their jovial guide controls the entire [[Sand Gulper]] supply chain to [[Dithoria City]], or that the crew helping them haul in their catch could have their ship stripped to the hull if Gary gave the word.

## Motives

Gary wants legitimacy — not instead of the piracy, but alongside it. He has cultivated relationships with some of the wealthiest families in [[Dithoria City]] through the tours and believes there is a version of the [[Gatorpaw Gang]] that operates openly as a premium fishery and charter company, with the less legal operations running quietly underneath. He is patient about this. He is building something.

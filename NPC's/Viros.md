# Viros

## Description

A pale [[Elf]] in a long dark-red coat — or perhaps it is just the blood. He carried a rod on his belt that appeared to have fingers along its length, a tentacle-like magic item that served as both weapon and conduit. He moved with the ease of someone who had not been surprised in a very long time.

Viros was an extremely powerful wizard who conducted experiments on the civilians of [[Frostcrag Glade]], using [[Ulthite]] mined from the [[Agite Mountains]] to bend people to his will. He controlled his reanimated subjects with precise, puppet-like magic — each one an extension of his will, moving only when and how he wanted.

## What He Did

- Destroyed [[The Red Talons]] at [[Frostcrag Glade]], killing most of [[Vel'Korr]]'s crew including [[Varnak]] the Red
- Raided the [[Abbey of the Silver Veil]], taking [[Mother Elyra]] — [[Faefi]]'s surrogate mother
- Used controlled festival-goers and the [[Tidemother]] Gate Stone to conduct a ritual at [[Coffin Rock]] during [[History/The Tidecrest Tournament of 1190|Tidescrest]]

## His End

Viros was confronted by [[The Salty Bitches]] in the excavation chamber beneath [[Coffin Rock]]. [[Faefi]] pushed him into the pit using [[Captain Bubblebeard]]'s hand. The Gate Stone shattered. He did not survive.

## Notable Relationships

- [[Zora Meadowlark]] — worked in service of her plan to retrieve the Gate Stones
- [[Mother Elyra]] — controlled and used as a lure
- [[The Red Talons]] — destroyed by him at Frostcrag Glade

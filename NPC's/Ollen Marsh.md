# [[Ollen Marsh]]

## Description

A tall, composed [[Human]] man in a dark uniform that has never once been wrinkled. He remembers every face that has come through [[The Black Bar]] and forgets nothing — orders, conversations, who sat with whom and for how long. He smiles with complete professionalism and reveals absolutely nothing behind it.

Ollen is the public face of [[The Blackwake Company]]'s operation on [[The Seat of Thalmyre]]. He controls who gets past the bar, who gets shown to the private back room, and what the Company knows about its clientele. He is not merely a bartender.

## Relationship to Party

Unknown — if the party enters The Black Bar, he is polite, attentive, and quietly cataloguing them.

## Notable Relationships

- [[The Blackwake Company]] — loyal employee, possibly more than that

## Location

Behind the bar at [[The Black Bar]], [[The Seat of Thalmyre]].

## Motives

To run a flawless establishment and serve the Company's interests without ever appearing to do so.

# [[Skit Blastwick]]

## Description

A small, soot-stained [[Goblin]] woman who is almost entirely deaf from occupational reasons and completely unbothered by this. She communicates via a combination of shouting, pointing, and an uncanny ability to read lips. She has no eyebrows — they grew back twice and she stopped bothering. Her apron has survived things that walls have not.

Skit knows more about explosive compounds than anyone in [[The Seat of Thalmyre]] and possibly [[Korlornium]]. She is cheerful, safety-indifferent, and deeply proud of her work. The [[The Rattails]] are her best customers. She does not ask what things are for and considers this a professional courtesy.

## Relationship to Party

Neutral — happy to sell to anyone with coin.

## Notable Relationships

- [[The Rattails]] — enthusiastic repeat customers

## Location

Runs [[The Blowpipe]] on the main drag of [[The Seat of Thalmyre]].

## Motives

To make the finest explosive devices in [[Korlornium]] and sell them to people who will use them interestingly.

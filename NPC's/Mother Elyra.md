# [[Mother Elyra]]

## Description

The former leader of the [[Abbey of the Silver Veil]] in the [[Agite Mountains]]. A stern but deeply warm [[Aasimar]] woman who ran the abbey with high standards and genuine love for those in her care. She treated [[Faefi]] as a daughter — the one person who never tried to cage the storm in her, only taught it to dance.

[[Viros]] raided the abbey and took her. [[Faefi]] never saw her die.

She is now reanimated and under [[Viros]]'s control. She is almost perfectly herself — warm, unhurried, calls [[Faefi]] by name. But something is slightly wrong in the way she moves, the way she doesn't blink quite right, the way she doesn't ask a single question she should be desperate to ask.

## As a Lure

[[Viros]] uses her to draw [[Faefi]] — and through her, the party — to [[Itchy Finger]]. She appears in [[Whelk's Landing]] during [[Tidescrest]], finds Faefi in the crowd, and tells her she escaped, that she tracked the party down, that she knows where Viros is headed. She is convincing. She is meant to be.

> [!Note]+
> The tell: she doesn't ask about the abbey. She doesn't ask who else survived. She doesn't ask if Faefi is okay. She just moves them toward the door.

## Relationship to Party

- [[Faefi]] — surrogate mother. The most important person in Faefi's life, and the one Faefi never got to grieve properly.

## Notable Relationships

- [[Viros]] — her killer and puppeteer

## Location

Reanimated and operating under [[Viros]]'s control in [[The Seat of Thalmyre]] during [[Tidescrest]].

## Motives

None of her own. She is a weapon shaped like someone Faefi loves.

# [[Tiamat]]

The oldest and most powerful dragon in [[Erodas]], [[Tiamat]] is said to have been the first creature born from the volcanic **Cradle of Scales** at the heart of [[Dracofold]]. She does not merely rule the continent — she is its apex predator, its god, and its warden all at once.

## Description

[[Tiamat]] is a colossal multi-headed dragon whose five heads each breathe a different elemental force, representing the five chromatic dragon lineages she mothered. She rarely moves from her seat at the [[Cradle of Scales]], but her reach extends across all of [[Dracofold]] through a network of chromatic dragon lieutenants who govern its vassal territories in her name.

Her will is law. Chromatic dragons who defy her are exiled — many who now terrorize other regions of [[Erodas]] are her castoffs, too powerful to destroy and too dangerous to keep near.

## Motives

[[Tiamat]] seeks to consolidate all draconic power under her control and expand [[Dracofold]]'s economic reach into the wider world. The [[Cinderbloom]] trade is her instrument — a flow of wealth that funds her military and lines her lieutenants' hoards. She has no interest in conquest by claw when commerce and fear will do the same work more quietly.

She banned the [[Dragonborn]] **Ashen Communion** not out of cultural hatred but out of cold economics. Every crystal of [[Cinderbloom]] drunk in ritual is a crystal not sold.

## Relationship to Party

Not yet encountered directly. Her influence is felt through the [[Cinderbloom]] supply chain.
